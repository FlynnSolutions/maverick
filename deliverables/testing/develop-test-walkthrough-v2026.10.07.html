Ship days on the calendar (#1)

A cadence puts ship days on the calendar going forward
Steps
  1. Open Maverick on the Maverick project and switch to the Calendar view.
  2. In the Ship days row under the releases strip, pick "every 2 weeks" and set the date to this coming Friday.
  3. Page forward two months.
Expected
  • A 🛫 ship day sits on that Friday and on every second Friday after it.
  • The row reads "next " for the first one.
  • The legend at the top of the grid shows the ship-day marker.
Dragging a ship day moves it and the drawer remembers where it came from
Steps
  1. Drag the next 🛫 ship day onto a different day in the same month.
  2. Click the moved ship day.
Expected
  • The marker lands on the new day and the old day is empty.
  • The drawer says "Pushed back from " (or "Pulled forward") with a "put it back" link.
  • "put it back" returns it to the cadence day.
Cancelling one cadence day leaves the cadence running
Steps
  1. Click a cadence ship day and press "cancel this ship day"; confirm.
  2. Look at the later ship days.
Expected
  • Only that day comes off the calendar; the ones after it stay.
  • Adding a one-off on the same date (next case) brings it back as the cadence day, not as a duplicate.
A one-off ship day outside the cadence
Steps
  1. In the Ship days row, pick a date that is not on the cadence in the one-off picker and press "add a one-off".
  2. Click it.
Expected
  • It appears on that day with the same marker.
  • Its drawer says "one-off" where a cadence day says the cadence words.
Marking a day shipped keeps it for good
Setup

Any ship day on the calendar.

Steps
  1. Click a ship day and press "mark shipped".
  2. Clear the cadence (set the row to "no cadence").
Expected
  • The day reads 🛬 shipped with the version, in green.
  • After clearing the cadence the other ship days vanish and the shipped one stays.
The banner finds you: today, and a missed day
Steps
  1. Add a one-off ship day on today's date. Switch to the Board view, then the Workspace view.
  2. Remove it. Add a one-off on yesterday's date and reload.
Expected
  • Today: a banner above every view says "Ship day. Planned for v" with "start the ship", "mark shipped" and "push it back".
  • Yesterday, unmarked: a red banner says the ship day went by without being marked.
  • "push it back" opens the day's drawer.

A milestone is walked before the mission lands (#2)

A merged milestone commissions its walkthrough
Setup

This pillar needs a mission with real agents. Defer it if you are not flying one today; the gate itself is under test (42 tests).

Steps
  1. Fly a small mission on a fixture project (one milestone, one or two tasks) and let the Wingmen and RIOs finish.
  2. When the milestone merges, open the mission page.
Expected
  • The milestone's nav badge reads "merged · walkthrough on its way".
  • The milestone page shows a Walkthrough panel naming the builder session, with a "watch" button that mounts its terminal.
  • The next milestone, if any, is dispatched; nothing waits on the walkthrough.
The document is hosted and the answers count
Steps
  1. Wait for the builder to commit the document (deliverables/testing/-m1-walkthrough.html on the mission branch).
  2. Answer the cases in the hosted panel.
Expected
  • The panel shows the document in a frame with pillars and a progress bar.
  • The nav badge reads "walk it · N of M" and climbs as you answer.
  • When every case is answered the badge reads "walked" and the panel header shows a passed verdict.
The review gate holds until every milestone is walked
Steps
  1. With a milestone merged but not walked, open "What the mission built".
Expected
  • No close button. The copy says how many milestones are still to walk and why.
  • A "walk milestone N" button per unwalked milestone jumps to its page.
Waiving is allowed, with a reason that is kept
Steps
  1. On an unwalked milestone, press "waive it" and give a reason.
  2. Return to the review page.
Expected
  • The milestone reads "merged · waived" in the nav and the review page shows "waived".
  • The reason is shown on the milestone page.
  • The close button is offered.
A waiver without a reason is refused
Steps
  1. Press "waive it" and submit an empty or blank reason.
Expected
  • The status line says to say why; nothing changes.

Planned is a field that names a real file, and the CAG (#3)

The board badges roadmap items that have no plan
Steps
  1. Open the Realtime project's board.
  2. Look at the Priority and In Progress lanes, then the Backlog.
Expected
  • Items without a plan: field naming a real file carry a red "unplanned" chip in Priority and In Progress.
  • Backlog items carry no such chip whatever their fields say.
  • Mission tasks (items with a mission: field) are not badged.
A resolving plan is a link
Steps
  1. Open the drawer of a Realtime item whose plan: field names a real file (for example the shared-board-groups plan).
  2. Click the plan value.
Expected
  • The plan row shows the field as a link.
  • It opens the plan document in a new tab, served from inside the project.
A plan field that names nothing reads red
Steps
  1. Edit any backlog item's markdown to add " - plan: plans/does-not-exist.md" and save.
  2. Reopen its drawer.
Expected
  • The plan row shows the value in red, not as a link.
  • Remove the line afterwards.
Spawn on an unplanned item offers the plan instead
Steps
  1. On a Priority item with the unplanned chip, press "spawn" on the card.
Expected
  • A dialog titled "This item has no plan" with a "plan it" verb, not the spawn dialog.
  • Pressing "plan it" opens a terminal titled "CAG · plan: " in the dock.
  • The drawer's spawn button is not the primary verb on this item; "plan it" is.
The CAG has no shell
Steps
  1. In that CAG terminal, ask it to run a command ("run ls" or "run the tests").
  2. Ask it to read the rulebook.
Expected
  • It says it cannot run commands: Bash is not among its tools.
  • It reads the file and answers from it.
A brainstorm is one dated document
Steps
  1. On the Workspace view, press "brainstorm" on the Missions rack, give a topic, and talk for a few turns.
  2. Ask it where it wrote the document.
Expected
  • A terminal titled "CAG · " opens.
  • A file deliverables/brainstorms/-.md exists in the project and grows as the conversation goes.
  • The tracker is not touched.
A plan written by the CAG makes the item planned
Steps
  1. Let the CAG in pl4 finish: answer its interview briefly and let it write the plan and set the field.
  2. Reload the board and open the item's drawer.
Expected
  • The unplanned chip is gone.
  • The plan row is a link to plans/-.md beside the tracker.
  • "spawn" is the primary verb again, and pressing it opens the spawn dialog.
