# Moments

What each surface needs from a person, and what the person needs back. Written before the actions
table; the rules are `ui-design/reference/moments.md` in flynn-kit.

| Moment | They arrive knowing | The ask, in its own words | What they need to decide | Where they answer | What comes next |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| The Strike Lead asks | "something is waiting on me", from the inbox, the tab title or the lamp | The question it wrote, as the card's heading | The question's context on the card; the message that led to it, one press away | On the card at the top of the mission page: options, own words, send | The card says the answer went and that it will write back; then that it wrote back, with the way to the conversation |
| A round the mission moved past | nothing: it is no longer asked, so no inbox entry, lamp or card points at it | None: the plan fired or the work finished after it was asked, so nothing waits on it | Whether it still matters, from its questions, folded where it was asked | Nothing to answer; the composer, if it still matters | The round reads "asked, overtaken" and by what, so it is neither waiting nor gone |
| A plan is ready to fly | "the plan is ready" | "The plan is ready to fly" | What it writes, spawns and costs, in the gate | The gate's approve, under those costs; the link at the top goes there | The status says the first milestone is out |
| A plan was sent back and is fixed | "the plan is ready", with Maverick's earlier send-back still in the thread | Nothing: the send-back is history, marked "fixed" and folded | That the plan in the gate is the one that passes; the old problems only if they want them | Nowhere: the fire is the ready moment above | The ready moment |
| A milestone is ready to walk | "ready to review" | "ready to review: walk milestone N, then close" | How far each walk has got, on its row | The walkthrough the row's button opens | The next milestone, then the close |
| A mission is in the air | "a mission is flying", or nothing: the app reopens on the last view, which may be the Board or the Calendar | Nothing asks; the page says it is there: the mission's name, its lamp and its word | Whether to go and look | The mission's link at the top of the Board and the Calendar, and in the Missions row of every workspace tab; on its crew tab, the mission's own strip above Oversight | The mission page, in one press |
| A mission is ready to close | "every milestone is walked or waived" | "ready to close" | The report, in the second gate | The gate's close, asked twice | It says what closing did |
