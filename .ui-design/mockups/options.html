The inbox: every mission and session grouped by what it needs from you

Superset (In progress / Ready for review, diff stats per row) · Conductor (see at a glance, then review and merge) · Linear (waiting for input as a state)

What you see first
Four groups, top to bottom: Needs you, Working, Ready to review, Done. Each row is one task or milestone with its diff stats. This is the workspace.
A question
Is a row in Needs you with an Answer button. Answer it without opening anything.
After it lands
The mission moves to Done; click it for the session page (option 1).
Cost to build
Low. The workspace already has racks sorted by urgency; this renames and regroups them.
keep

The most logical front door: the top of the page is always what you have to do. It is a list, not a story; it needs option 1 behind it to say why.

The thread: a mission is a conversation

Claude Code on the web (a session is a chat; questions wait in it) · Factory (talk to the orchestrator in plain language) · Jules (plan inline, approve in the thread)

What you see first
A chat. The plan is a message with an Approve button. Progress is messages. A question is a message you reply to.
A question
The newest message, with buttons, and a reply box for anything else.
After it lands
The last message is the summary with the PR. Scroll up for history.
Cost to build
Lowest. One list of messages and a reply box.
no, on its own

Nothing is easier to follow for one agent. With nine tasks in the air it becomes a firehose, and the plan scrolls out of sight the moment work starts. Worth stealing the reply box.

What I would build: 3 in front of 1, with 4's reply box

The workspace is the inbox (3). The top group is always what needs you, answerable in place. Click any row and you land on the mission's session page (1), scrolled to that task. The session page has four fixed anchors and never changes shape, before, during or after the flight. At the bottom of Progress sits a reply box to the Strike Lead (4), so "pause and tell it what you see" is one line, not a form. Mission Control (2) is not a page; it is what Progress looks like while the mission is flying, if you ever want the three-pane view, and it can come later.

  1. Workspace = Needs you · Working · Ready to review · Done. Rows carry diff stats and a lamp.
  2. Mission page = The ask · Plan · Progress · Result, left rail, one status word.
  3. A question appears in both places with the same buttons, and in a macOS notification.
  4. Reply box at the bottom of Progress, to the Strike Lead.
