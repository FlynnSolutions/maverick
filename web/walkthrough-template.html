Journeys

What a person does across the whole stack, start to end: a screen, what the server does with it, what is stored, and the screen again.

j1Picking a station shows its tides from the feed, and they survive a reload

Steps
  1. Open the app and pick the station "Pier 7".
  2. Read the next high tide on the chart and in the table.
  3. Reload the page.
Expected
  • The chart and the table show the same next high tide, to the minute.
  • The table header names the datum it uses.
  • After the reload the same station and the same times show, without a second fetch (the network panel shows none).

At scale

The same screens under a large data set. The command makes it from invented data and takes it away again.

s1Five thousand stations: the picker stays quick and the chart still draws

Setup
node tools/seed.mjs --stations 5000 --days 30
Steps
  1. Open the station picker and type "pier".
  2. Pick the last station in the list and open its chart.
Expected
  • The list narrows as you type, with no pause you can feel.
  • The chart draws all 30 days without the page freezing.
Afterwards
node tools/seed.mjs --clear
